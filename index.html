⏰ PART 1 — TIME
Telling the time · Time expressions · Prepositions of time

🕐 Asking & Telling the Time

Hỏi giờ: What time is it? · Do you have the time? · Could you tell me what time it is?
o'clock: It's one o'clock = 1:00
half past: It's half past 4 = 4:30 (bốn giờ rưỡi)
a quarter past: It's a quarter past 4 = 4:15 (bốn giờ mười lăm phút)
a quarter to: It's a quarter to 4 = 3:45 (bốn giờ kém mười lăm)
A.M. / P.M.: It's 7 A.M. (buổi sáng) · It's 3 P.M. (buổi chiều)
🕰️ Đọc đồng hồ — Chọn đáp án đúng

Nhìn đồng hồ, chọn cách đọc giờ đúng.

⚠️ Time Prepositions — Giới từ chỉ thời gian hay nhầm

until vs by: I'll be here until 4:30 (tôi ở đây đến tận 4:30) · I'll be there by 8:15 (tôi sẽ có mặt không muộn hơn 8:15)
for vs since: I've worked here for six months (khoảng thời gian) · I've worked here since May (điểm thời gian)
during vs for: I worked during the summer (khi nào) · I worked for a month (bao lâu)
on days: on Monday · on Saturday evening · I saw her on Friday
in months/seasons: in July · in spring · My birthday is in July ❌ NOT on July
at the weekend: I went to the cinema at the weekend ❌ NOT in the weekend
📐 Counting Time — Đơn vị thời gian
⏱

🕐

📅

📆

💡 Fortnight /ˈfɔːt.naɪt/ = hai tuần  |  Decade /ˈdek.eɪd/ = thập kỷ (10 năm)  |  Millennium /mɪˈlen.i.əm/ = 1000 năm

📅 Days, Months & Seasons

Days: Sunday · Monday · Tuesday · Wednesday · Thursday · Friday · Saturday (always capital)
Months: January · February · March · April · May · June · July · August · September · October · November · December
Seasons: spring 🌸 · summer ☀️ · autumn/fall 🍂 · winter ❄️ (no capital)
Saturday + Sunday = the weekend
We say on + day: on Monday, on Saturday · in + month/season: in July, in spring
💬 Useful Time Expressions
in time — kịp giờ
We got there in time.
on time — đúng giờ
My brother is always on time.
have time — có đủ thời gian
Do you have time for coffee?
next time / last time
Call me the next time you're in London.
in a moment — trong chốc lát
I'll be with you in a moment.
at the moment — ngay lúc này
Nora's in Paris at the moment.
once/twice a week
I go swimming once a week.
recently / soon / ago
I got up four hours ago.

💬 Asking about Weather

What's the weather like? = How's the weather? (hai câu tương đương)
What kind of weather do you like? → I like / I don't like …
What's the weather like in your country in June? → It's usually warm and sunny.
It's a lovely day. / It's a horrible day, isn't it!
It's foggy in Sydney and it's snowing in Moscow.
It's raining in Barcelona but the sun is shining in Granada.
